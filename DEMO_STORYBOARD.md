**Ghost Protocol — Your AI can propose. You decide.** Show the spectacular but truthful Decision Rail within the first 8 seconds. Video must show actual app running and explain Nebius and NVIDIA.

## Capture instructions
- Record 1920×1080 at 30 fps (or high quality equivalent), crop only the app; no tabs exposing personal data or credentials.
- Screen text must be readable at 720p; zoom browser if needed.
- Use original narration/music or appropriately licensed audio. Do not overfill with transitions.
- No fake tool-execution footage. If showing the four live Nemotron results, label as previous isolated live-provider verification, not frontend live run.
- Keep final YouTube video PUBLIC and under 3 minutes. Verify link in logged-out/incognito mode.

## One-line pitch
“Ghost Protocol turns personal AI from an unchecked actor into a proposed, approved and auditable workflow.”
